Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 July 2026

This policy explains what personal information Flood International, LLC, a California limited liability company ("Flood International", "we", "us"), collects through floodinternational.com, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Flood International, LLC, a California LLC located in Los Angeles, California. Questions about this policy: king@floodinternational.com.

2. Personal information we collect

Information you provide

When you submit an inquiry form or book a call, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you provide it
  • Your brand or company name
  • The service tier you are interested in
  • Anything else you choose to include in your responses

Information collected automatically

Our host and the third-party widgets embedded on this site receive technical data including your IP address, browser type, device information, and the pages you view. See our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a call
  • To follow up if you begin an inquiry and do not finish it
  • To provide and administer our services if you become a client
  • To send billing and account communications
  • To meet our tax, accounting, and legal obligations

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We disclose it to the following service providers, who process it on our behalf and under contract:

5. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that do not become clients: 24 months from last contact.
  • Client records: for the term of the engagement plus 4 years, to meet tax and accounting requirements.
  • Email correspondence: 24 months.

6. Your choices

You may request that we correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask what we hold, by emailing king@floodinternational.com. We will respond within 45 days. You may opt out of marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link.

We do not currently meet the thresholds that make the California Consumer Privacy Act apply to us. We honor the requests above regardless, as a matter of practice. If our size changes such that the CCPA applies, we will update this policy.

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no industry standard for responding to it, and this site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. We do not permit third parties to collect personally identifiable information about your activity across other websites.

8. Artificial intelligence

Our services use artificial intelligence to draft and process material. Where AI is used on information you provide, it supports human decisions rather than replacing them, and a person reviews the output. AI-generated output may contain errors and does not constitute legal, financial, investment, or tax advice.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, and reputable service providers. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you as required by California Civil Code § 1798.82.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date above, and we will notify clients directly where the law requires it.
