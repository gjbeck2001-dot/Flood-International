Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This page explains which are used on floodinternational.com, by whom, and how to control them.

1. Essential cookies

These are required for the site to function and do not need your consent. Our host, Vercel, may set cookies for security and load balancing.

2. Third-party services on this site

We embed three third-party services. Each may set cookies or transmit your IP address to its provider when the page loads.

ServicePurposeWhat it does
Calendly Call booking Sets cookies to manage the booking widget and remember your session
Tally Inquiry forms Sets cookies to manage form state and prevent duplicate submissions
Google Fonts Typography Fonts are requested from Google's servers, which receives your IP address

Their own privacy policies govern what they do with the data they collect: Calendly, Tally, Google.

We do not permit these providers to collect personally identifiable information about your activity across other websites over time.

3. What we do not use

We do not run advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or analytics that profile you across other websites.

4. Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking them may stop the booking and inquiry forms from working. Blocking requests to Google's font servers may change how the site looks but will not break it.

5. Do Not Track

This site does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because no common standard for honoring them exists.
