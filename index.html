Questions

Fair questions.

Price and terms

How much does it cost?

There are four plans: Follow-Up at $1,500 a month, Content at $1,500 a month, Growth at $3,000 a month and Full at $5,000 a month. No setup fee on any of them.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. It's month to month, with no minimum term.

How do I cancel?

Email cancel@floodinternational.com at any time. We will confirm in writing, and service runs to the end of the month you have paid for. There is no cancellation fee. If an invoice for a later month isn't paid by its due date, we may treat that as a cancellation: service ends at the end of the last month you paid for, and nothing is charged for the unpaid month.

Do I get a refund if I cancel?

Fees already paid for the current billing period are not refunded, and services continue through the end of that period.

Do you offer discounts?

No. The price moves only when the scope moves, so a lower price always means a smaller plan. We decline rather than discount.

How do I pay?

After you sign, you get a secure Stripe checkout link for the first month. Later months are invoiced.

Can you promise results?

No one can honestly guarantee revenue, bookings or followers, so we don't. What we commit to is the work: the numbers in your plan, every month.

Full adds 90 pieces of content a month in place of 60 from the same monthly shoot, more prospects, a text back on missed calls through a dedicated tracking number after carrier registration and a review every 2 weeks.
