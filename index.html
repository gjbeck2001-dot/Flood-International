01 / THE PROBLEM

You're on the tools. The phone keeps ringing. Nobody picks up.

A call comes in while you're under a house. It goes to voicemail, and the customer calls the next shop on Google. A booked customer goes quiet or cancels, and nobody asks them to rebook. A finished job never gets a review request or a reminder for next season. None of it shows up on a report. It just never turns into revenue.

02 / WHAT IT DOES

The front-office work that slips when you're busy. Handled every day, on your Jobber account.

01 / MISSED CALLS

A text back in seconds.

When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text from your business number asking what they need. Their answer lands in Jobber as a new request.

02 / BOOKINGS

Confirmed, with the tech's name.

Customers get a confirmation when the visit is booked, then a reminder the day before to reply YES or NO. No reply gets a second text, then a call task for your office. Nothing auto-cancels.

03 / RESCHEDULES

A "no" worked back to a booking.

When a customer can't make it, they get offered a new time and a booking link right away, before they forget or call someone else.

04 / JOB REPORTS

Drafted for your office.

When a visit is marked complete, a clean report is drafted from the tech's notes and photos. Your office approves it from a link before it goes to the customer.

05 / REVIEWS + REPEAT WORK

The follow-up nobody has time for.

Every customer gets a review request the day after. A low score triggers a same-day call-back task for the owner. Unpaid invoices get a reminder, and past customers hear from you when a tune-up is due.

06 / REPORTING

One number every month.

A monthly report shows the jobs recovered and what they were worth. Plus a customer report: your best customers, your top zip codes, and who's due for service.

Texts go out in English and Spanish. Emergency replies like "gas smell" or "burst pipe" page your on-call tech within a minute.

06 / HOW IT WORKS

One call to start. Nothing new for your techs to learn.

STEP 1
We talk.

A 30-minute call. We look at how calls, bookings and follow-up work in your shop today, and where jobs slip through.

STEP 2
We connect.

You fill in one onboarding form: hours, techs, on-call roster, review link. We connect Jobber and forward unanswered calls to your texting line. You keep your number, and calls still ring your shop first.

STEP 3
You go live.

US carriers have to register every business texting line. We file it the day you sign, and texting turns on once it's approved, usually 1 to 3 weeks.

EVERY MONTH
You see the number.

Your report lands on the 3rd business day of every month: jobs recovered, revenue recovered, and how this month compares with the last 3, 6 and 12.
